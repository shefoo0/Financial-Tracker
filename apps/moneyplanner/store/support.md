# Money Planner support

Need help or want to report a problem? Email CONTACT_EMAIL. Please say which phone and Android or iOS version you use, and what you tapped before the problem. Do not send your backup file or screenshots that show private amounts unless we ask.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?** Only on your phone. There is no account and no server. That also means we cannot recover your data for you, so please keep a backup file (Settings, then Backup).

**How do I move to a new phone?** On the old phone, save a backup file in Settings and send it to yourself. On the new phone, install the app and restore from that file.

**How do I back up?** The app keeps automatic copies for the last 7 days. For your own safe copy, save a backup file from Settings and store it somewhere you trust.

**How do I get my data out?** Settings has Export to spreadsheet (CSV).

**I forgot my PIN.** The PIN is not stored in a readable form and we cannot reset it. If you have a backup file, email us and we will explain your options.

**Reminders do not appear.** Check that notifications are allowed for Money Planner in your phone settings. On some Android phones, battery saving can delay reminders: allow the app to run in the background.

**How do I delete my data?** Manage profiles, then Delete profile. Uninstalling the app also removes everything on the phone.

**Is this financial advice?** No. It is a tracking tool, and forecasts are estimates from what you entered.

**Pro.** Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Use Restore purchases in the app on a new phone with the same store account.
