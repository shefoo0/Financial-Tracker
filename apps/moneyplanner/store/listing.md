## App Store description

Money Planner shows where your money will be in the coming weeks and months, so a bill or a cheque never surprises you.

Add your salary, rent, loans and other bills once. Money Planner builds a forecast from them: a daily view for the next 60 days and a monthly view for the next 24 months, with your lowest balance and the date it happens.

HOME
- Balance today and the forecast line
- Safe to spend until payday, and about how much a day
- Coming up: the next bills, instalments, cheques and salary, with a one-tap Paid button
- Alerts in the app when the forecast drops below zero or below the buffer you choose

PLAN YOUR MONEY
- Recurring income and costs: monthly (a day or the last day), weekly, every 2 weeks, every N weeks or months, yearly
- Skip one payment, move it, change its amount or mark it paid, without touching the rest
- One-time items in a month view you can swipe through
- Categories you can rename, plus an optional monthly limit per category
- Fast entry with recent items and your last category

ACCOUNTS AND CARDS
- Your balance is the total of your bank and cash accounts; tap it to see each one
- Card purchases count as spending when you make them and add to what you owe; the bank pays the card on its due date, and the payment is not counted twice
- Move money between your own accounts without it counting as spending or income

DEBTS, CARDS AND CHEQUES
- Loans with payment day and end date
- Credit cards with limit, balance and available credit, and a repayment plan the forecast follows
- Instalment and buy-now-pay-later plans with editable payment dates
- Post-dated cheques, including a helper for a series such as rent
- Partial and extra payments
- Debt-to-income percentage and the debt-free date

REMINDERS
- Notifications on your phone for upcoming bills, instalments and cheques, and for forecast alerts
- You choose the lead time, and switch them on or off
- Notifications are created on the device; nothing is sent from a server

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account and no sign-up
- No bank link: you type items in, or import a statement file
- Works offline, with no ads and no tracking
- Your data is stored on your phone only
- A PIN lock for each profile, with fingerprint or Face ID if your phone has it
- Profiles for a partner or a shared household, each with its own data and lock
- Automatic local backups of the last 7 days, and a backup file you can save wherever you choose
- Export your data to a spreadsheet (CSV)

PRO: ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Planning, forecast, debts, cheques, alerts, profiles, lock and backups are free. Pro is a single purchase, not a subscription:
- Quarterly and yearly reports: income, spending, saved, savings rate, cash flow, debts and key figures compared with earlier periods, with a PDF you can share
- Statement import from CSV, Excel, OFX, PDF, photos and shared text. Reading happens on your phone and the files never leave it
- Several bank and cash accounts, the account of each item and a default account, and statements imported straight into an account or onto a card

Made with the UAE in mind: AED by default, cheques, card instalments and monthly salary. Money Planner is a tracking tool and does not give financial advice. It is not a bank, does not lend money and does not hold your money.

## App Store privacy label

**Data Not Collected.** The app has no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and makes no network calls of its own. Data stays on the device. ## Google Play Data safety

Checked in the code on 2026-11-23: a search for fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket and http(s) URLs in `src/` found no network calls in the app (the one `fetch` is in `files.web.ts`, the web build's reader for a file the user picked in the browser; it is not in the phone app). Libraries used: expo-sqlite, expo-secure-store, expo-notifications (local only), expo-local-authentication, expo-sharing, expo-document-picker, expo-image-picker (camera and photo picker), expo-print, expo-file-system, expo-text-extractor (on-device text recognition: Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iOS), expo-pdf-text-extract and react-native-pdf-thumbnail (PDF text and pages, on the device). The ML Kit text model comes with Google Play services on the phone; the app makes no request of its own.

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no data collected, no transmission). If the console forces an answer: Yes / not applicable.
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Not applicable (no data leaves the device). In the app, a profile and all its data can be deleted in Manage profiles, and uninstalling removes everything.
- Data types, each marked **Not collected / Not shared**: location, personal info, financial info, health and fitness, messages, photos and videos, audio, files and docs, calendar, contacts, app activity, web browsing, app info and performance, device or other IDs.
- Why "financial info" is not collected: the amounts, bills and balances the user types are kept only in a database on the phone and are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Google's definition of "collect" is transmitting data off the device.
- What the app does on the device (for the privacy policy, not Data safety fields): notifications are scheduled locally; the PIN is stored as a salted hash in the phone's secure storage; fingerprint and face unlock are handled by the operating system and the app only receives yes or no; the user may share a backup, CSV or PDF file through the system share sheet, which is the user's own action; imported statement files and photos are read on the device.
- Pro purchase (checked 2026-11-24): made through Google Play Billing (expo-iap 6.4.2, MIT, no server of its own, no API key, none of its optional paid services set up). The app asks Google Play whether Pro is owned and keeps a yes/no flag in the phone's secure storage; nothing is sent to the developer, so purchase history is **not collected** by the app (Google Play handles it under its own policy). Permission added by Play Billing: `com.android.vending.BILLING`.
