# Money Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Money Planner ("the app") is a personal money planner. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer does not receive your data and cannot see it. The app does not connect to your bank.

## What the app stores on your phone

Everything you enter, in a database inside the app on your phone: profile names, your balance, income, bills, one-time items, categories, loans, cards, instalment plans, cheques, payment history and settings. Each profile has its own separate data.

If you turn on the lock for a profile, the app keeps a salted hash of your PIN (not the PIN itself) in your phone's secure storage.

The app also keeps automatic backup copies of the last 7 days on your phone, inside the app's own storage.

## Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules reminders and alerts on your phone. They are created on the device from your own data. They are not sent from a server, and nothing about them is sent to us.

## Fingerprint and Face ID

If you choose biometric unlock, your phone's operating system checks your fingerprint or face. The app only gets a yes or no answer. It never receives or stores your biometric data.

## Files you create or choose

- Backups, spreadsheet (CSV) exports and PDF reports are files you create. They are shared only when you pick where to send or save them with your phone's share sheet. After that, the file is under your control and the app cannot manage it. Keep backup files somewhere safe: they contain your money data.
- If you import a statement (a file, a photo or shared text), the app reads it on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere.
- Restoring a backup reads the file you choose.

## Purchases

The Pro unlock is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the App Store. They process your payment under their own policies. The app does not see your payment details.

## Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data, because we do not have it.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a profile and all its data in the app (Manage profiles). A safety copy of a deleted profile is kept on your phone for 7 days, then removed. Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your phone, including automatic backups. Backup or export files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Children

The app is a finance tool for adults and is not directed at children.

## Not financial advice

Money Planner is a tracking tool. It does not give financial, legal or tax advice, is not a bank, and does not lend or hold money. Forecasts are estimates from the information you enter.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Changes that affect your data will be described in the app's release notes.
